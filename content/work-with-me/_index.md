  <h1>Let's Build Better Robotics Systems</h1>

Hi I'm Jasmeet Singh 👋,

an Independent Robotics Engineer specializing in Robotics Simulations, Autonomy, Systems and Infrastructure.

Over the past few years, I've worked across industrial AMRs, robotics simulation platforms, controls, motion planning, robotics software infrastructure and ROS 1 → ROS 2 migration,. My work spans from real-world development, deployment and debugging of autonomous systems to simulation-driven development, testing, and developer tooling.

I'm also actively involved in the open-source ROS and Gazebo ecosystem through upstream Gazebo contributions (including contributions as a [GSoC student](https://www.openrobotics.org/blog/2023/6/14/2023-google-summer-of-code-students) with Open Robotics), technical talks, community initiatives (Co-founded [A.T.O.M. Robotics Lab](https://github.com/atom-robotics-lab)), and open source projects.

I particularly enjoy solving systems-level robotics problems where autonomy, simulation, infrastructure, and real-world deployment intersect.
